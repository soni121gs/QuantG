# Grinold-Kahn Fundamental Law

Core idea: information ratio scales with skill times breadth: `IR = IC * sqrt(BR)`.

QuantG use: prefer many independent, falsifiable bets across names, events, regimes, and horizons over repeatedly tuning one narrow options setup.

Research rule: a candidate sleeve must state expected breadth, likely correlation to existing sleeves, and whether it improves portfolio heat rather than only single-strategy P&L.

Kill criterion: reject designs that get their apparent edge from many parameter trials on one dependent bet.
