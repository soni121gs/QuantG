# Hermes Hypothesis Card Rules

Core idea: Hermes may draft research, but code computes evidence and humans approve actions.

QuantG use: Phase 4 cards require citations from corpus notes or deterministic probes.

Research rule: each card must include hypothesis, who-pays rationale, universe, horizon, judge, data needed, and kill criteria.

Kill criterion: reject uncited cards or cards with no falsifiable judge.
