# Defined Risk First

Core idea: every strategy should know its maximum loss before it trades.

QuantG use: Phase 3 event and premium sleeves use condors, spreads, or deep-ITM controlled-risk vehicles.

Research rule: prefer structures with explicit max loss and margin clarity.

Kill criterion: reject naked short option designs unless founder explicitly approves a separate live-risk program.
