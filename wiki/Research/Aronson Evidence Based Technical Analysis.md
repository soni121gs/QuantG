# Aronson Evidence Based Technical Analysis

Core idea: technical ideas need statistical testing, multiple-comparison control, and clear null hypotheses.

QuantG use: Edge Lab DSR/trials-count gates and OOS-first ladder enforce this.

Research rule: no card may call a pattern useful until it survives out-of-sample and friction.

Kill criterion: reject narratives with no null hypothesis or no holdout.
