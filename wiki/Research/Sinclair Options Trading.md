# Sinclair Options Trading

Core idea: option structures are vehicles for expressing a measurable forecast, not strategies by themselves.

QuantG use: define the forecast first, then choose spread, condor, calendar, or single-leg vehicle that best fits payoff and cost.

Research rule: every hypothesis card must name the forecast, who pays, why now, and which judge can falsify it.

Kill criterion: reject vehicle-first ideas with no measurable premise.
