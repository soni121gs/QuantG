# SEBI FY25 F&O Study

Core idea: most retail F&O participation loses money after costs.

QuantG use: options strategies must assume friction and adverse selection are real, not small details.

Research rule: any strategy depending on retail flow must identify why QuantG is not the marginal loser paying spread, brokerage, and theta.

Kill criterion: reject low-credit/high-turnover ideas whose expected edge is below 3x friction.
