# Natenberg Option Volatility

Core idea: option value is governed by volatility, skew, term structure, and Greeks; strike selection must respect the entire surface.

QuantG use: IV surface, skew, and term richness feed seller gates and contract selection.

Research rule: short-vol ideas must explain whether edge comes from level, skew, term, event premium, or realized-vol mismatch.

Kill criterion: reject fixed-strike heuristics that ignore surface state and liquidity.
