# India PEAD Evidence

Core idea: post-earnings announcement drift may exist, but evidence is mixed and depends on surprise measurement.

QuantG use: P3-4 preliminary PEAD validator follows event-day direction as a weak proxy until surprise magnitude data exists.

Research rule: PEAD needs earnings date, event reaction, forward return, and eventually surprise magnitude.

Kill criterion: reject PEAD if event-reaction continuation is flat after costs.
