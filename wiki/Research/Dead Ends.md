# Dead Ends

This register exists so Hermes and future agents do not re-propose ideas QuantG already killed.

## Option Buyers

Status: dead across repeated studies.

Evidence: the current book census and historical validators repeatedly found option-buying variants negative after costs. Do not revive single-leg/debit buyer sleeves without a new OOS result that clears sample, cost, and DSR gates.

## Low-Credit Intraday Credit Geometry

Status: structurally unfundable.

Evidence: width-1 and low-delta credit spreads fail the cost-floor law in measured live-chain probes. The old geometry risked most or all collected credit to bank a small fraction, requiring unrealistic win rates.

## Clock-Driven Theta Recycling

Status: not theta harvesting unless TP reachability clears.

Evidence: most closed intraday spreads exited by clock instead of price trigger. A seller whose hold window cannot let decay deliver most of the take-profit is a directional coin flip paying friction.

## Iron Condor Infrastructure

Status: do not build live four-leg infra yet.

Evidence: prior condor research was fragile or unsupported; calendar/condor structures remain research-only until the judge produces a robust, cost-cleared OOS pass.

## GEX/OI NIFTY Entry Signal

Status: dead as direct NIFTY edge.

Evidence: prior IA work found GEX/OI did not provide a usable NIFTY entry edge. Treat OI only as a possible overlay after IC validation.

## FII/DII Cash Flow Signal

Status: dead as direct signal.

Evidence: long-window tests showed no reliable edge. Participant F&O OI is a different data source and still requires IC validation.

## BANKNIFTY Intraday Theta Seller

Status: infeasible under current intraday theta geometry.

Evidence: BANKNIFTY monthly expiry makes TP reachability too low for short intraday holds. Redesign would need multi-day hold logic and a fresh judge run.

## 0-DTE Intraday Credit Seller

Status: fails the cost floor.

Evidence: measured 0-DTE credit collapses below the 3x friction floor in tested geometries.
