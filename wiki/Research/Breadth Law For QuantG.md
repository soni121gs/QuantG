# Breadth Law For QuantG

Core idea: a book built from diverse weak-to-moderate edges can be more robust than one tuned strong-looking edge.

QuantG use: Phase 3 book assembly reports candidate count, heat, and correlation caps.

Research rule: prefer independent sleeves: event premium, daily trend, slow VRP, PEAD, and overlays only when each is proven.

Kill criterion: reject a book where all sleeves are the same NIFTY short-put bet.
