# Sinclair Volatility Trading

Core idea: short-volatility edge exists only when implied volatility is rich versus realized volatility after transaction costs and tail risk.

QuantG use: premium-selling sleeves must clear IV/RV richness, IV-surface richness, and 3x modeled friction before paper.

Research rule: sell options only when the buyer is plausibly overpaying for convexity.

Kill criterion: reject premium strategies that rely on high win rate while average loss overwhelms average win.
