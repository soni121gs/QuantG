# Carver Systematic Trading

Core idea: robust systems combine simple forecasts, diversification, volatility targeting, and conservative risk overlays.

QuantG use: daily delta-1 and multi-sleeve book assembly should prioritize stable forecast families and sizing discipline over aggressive entry rules.

Research rule: validate forecast, vehicle, sizing, and portfolio contribution separately.

Kill criterion: reject systems whose performance disappears after volatility targeting or correlation caps.
