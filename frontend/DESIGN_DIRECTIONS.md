# QuantG Frontend Design Directions

## 1. Control Room
Dense, tactical, and built for repeated trading decisions. This is the direction implemented on the shell and dashboard: quieter surfaces, stronger hierarchy, compact risk controls, and scan-first market panels.

## 2. Risk Desk
More conservative and compliance-oriented. Prioritize kill-switch state, margin pressure, broker connectivity, and audit trails. Best next step for live trading readiness.

## 3. Strategy Lab
Builder-first UI for research and iteration. Put strategy health, backtest snapshots, AI notes, and runtime settings into a split workspace. Best next step if you spend most time designing systems.

## 4. Mobile Command
Phone-first layout with bottom workflows, larger touch targets, simplified P&L state, and one-handed emergency controls. Best next step if this will be used during market hours away from desktop.
